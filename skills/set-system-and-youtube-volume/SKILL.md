---
name: set-system-and-youtube-volume
description: >-
  Sets a Mac's system volume to a target percentage and ensures a YouTube player's volume slider is at (or near) 100% without pausing or restarting playback. Use when you need consistent playback loudness for browser video/audio.
---

## Steps

1. Make the browser window active: click anywhere in the Chrome window that is playing the YouTube video (do not click the video play/pause control).
2. Set the YouTube player volume to maximum:
   - Click the speaker icon at the bottom-left of the YouTube player to reveal the player volume slider.
   - Drag the small white volume knob fully to the right (about 100% player volume). If the slider moves in increments, drag until it reaches the far-right end.
   - Leave the video playing; do not pause or reload the page.
3. Set the Mac system volume to the target (~45%):
   - Preferred: press the keyboard volume-up/down keys in short taps until the macOS on-screen volume indicator reads about 45%.
   - Alternate: click the Control Center icon in the menu bar, open Sound, and drag the system volume slider to about 45%.
4. Confirm:
   - Verify the YouTube player's volume slider remains at the rightmost position and the player's speaker icon is not muted.
   - Verify the macOS on-screen volume indicator or the Control Center slider shows ~45%.
   - Quickly listen to confirm playback level matches expectations; do not restart the video.

## Tips

- If the keyboard volume keys change system volume by fixed steps, use short, single taps to fine-tune to near 45% rather than holding the key.
- If the menu bar does not show Control Center, hold Option and click the speaker icon (or enable Control Center in System Settings) to access the slider.
- Avoid clicking the browser tab or reloading the page, which may pause/restart playback; click only the player controls or the menu bar for system volume.
- If the player slider is not visible (custom UI or overlays), click the video area once to show controls, then click the speaker icon.
