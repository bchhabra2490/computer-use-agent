---

## Steps
1. In Chrome, focus the address bar (Cmd+L) and navigate to: https://x.com/compose/post
2. Confirm you're signed in as the correct account (account handle should appear in the compose UI).
3. Click the compose text area and type or paste your post content (Cmd+V to paste).
4. (Optional) To attach media, click the media/attach button in the compose UI and select files.
5. Review the post and click the "Post"/"Tweet" button in the compose UI to publish.
6. Verify the post appears on your profile (https://x.com/bchhabra2490).
Notes: do not enter passwords; if not signed in, sign in through Chrome's normal authentication flow before composing.
