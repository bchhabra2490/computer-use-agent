  Verifies availability and reliability of spoilers/summaries for a specified manga chapter by checking official publishers (MangaPlus/Viz), fandom wikis, Reddit spoiler threads, and major anime/manga news sites; cross-checks details across at least two independent sources and then returns a short, source-checked spoiler summary or reports that no reputable spoilers exist. Use when asked to fetch chapter spoilers or short summaries for future manga chapters from a Mac desktop.
---

## Steps

1. Open Google Chrome on the Mac (Cmd+Space → type "Google Chrome" → Enter).
2. Focus the address bar (Cmd+L). Run the canonical search queries for the chapter, e.g.:
   - "<Series> <chapter number> spoiler"
   - "<Series> chapter <number> summary"
   - "Chapter <number> <Series> raw/scanlation"
   Replace <Series> and <number> as needed.
3. First check official release channels for the chapter:
   - Search and open MangaPlus and VIZ (Cmd+L → "MangaPlus <Series> <chapter>" / "VIZ <Series> <chapter>").
   - Confirm whether the chapter is published there and note the official release date/time.
4. Open a fandom summary/wiki page (One Piece Wiki or equivalent) and check the chapter entry for a summary and the displayed release status.
5. Open community spoiler threads (Reddit: r/<series>, r/<series>Spoilers) and at least one other independent source (another Reddit thread, a major anime news site, or a well-known fan-summary site).
   - Prefer threads with many upvotes/comments or posts by recognized spoiler accounts; check timestamps and moderator notes that indicate reliability.
6. Cross-check details across at least two independent sources (official release + one reputable community/news source OR two independent community sources if official not available).
   - Compare character actions, major events, revelations, and any cliffhanger. If sources disagree, note which details are corroborated by multiple independent posts and which are only claimed by single/unverified posts.
7. Decide reliability:
   - If MangaPlus/VIZ (or another official publisher) has the chapter: treat content as official and summarize confidently.
   - If only community posts/unverified leaks exist: label the chapter spoilers as "unverified" or "fan translations/scanlation claims" and only include details corroborated by multiple independent sources; otherwise report "no reputable spoilers available." Avoid using or linking to pirated raw/scan sites.
8. Produce a concise spoiler summary (a few paragraphs). Include:
   - Characters involved
   - Key actions and outcomes
   - Major revelations or twists
   - Any cliffhanger or setup for next chapter
   - If spoilers are unverified, prepend or append a short note stating their verification status.
   Return only the summary text (no links or extraneous commentary).

## Tips

- Prioritize official English releases (MangaPlus/VIZ) for accuracy; use wikis and major news sites as secondary verification.
- For Reddit, prefer threads with corroborating comments, screenshots from legitimate publishers, or translations by well-known translators; check timestamps relative to official release timezones.
- If a claimed "raw" or leak appears, do not visit illegal/pirated sites unnecessarily; rely on reputable community discussions about the leak instead.
- When in doubt, report "no reputable spoilers available yet." Always mark unverified claims clearly to the user.
- Keep the returned summary short and spoiler-focused (a few paragraphs). Do not include links, screenshots, or metadata in the final summary.
