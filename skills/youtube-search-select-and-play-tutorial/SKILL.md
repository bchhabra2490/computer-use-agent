## Steps

1. Open Google Chrome with Spotlight (Cmd+Space → type “Google Chrome” → Enter).
2. Go to https://www.youtube.com in the address bar.
3. Click the search box, type the query (e.g. “how to use a multimeter beginner voltage current resistance continuity”) and press Enter.
4. Click Filters (top-right of the search bar) → choose "Video" to hide Shorts/playlists.
5. Scroll the video results and inspect each thumbnail for channel name and duration (duration displayed bottom-right of thumbnail).
6. Prefer reputable channels. Pick a clear beginner-friendly video within the requested duration range (typically 3–10 minutes). If none from preferred channels match, choose the clearest tutorial that covers the requested measurements and range/probe usage.
7. Click the chosen thumbnail to open the video page.
8. Confirm playback is running (Pause icon visible or progress bar moving) and ensure audio is audible. Start playback if it doesn't auto-play (press spacebar or click Play).
9. Unmute the player: click the speaker icon on the video player or press M to toggle mute/unmute. If no audio, raise system output volume from the menu bar or use the "set-system-and-youtube-volume" skill.
10. No need to report anything back. Leave the video playing.

## Tips

- Use Filters → Video to avoid Shorts and playlists.
- Duration must be checked on thumbnails; if a tutorial is slightly outside the range but clearly concise, confirm with the user before selecting.
- Use M to quickly toggle YouTube mute/unmute. If Chrome audio is muted in macOS, open the volume control from the menu bar.
- If multiple matching videos appear, prefer channels listed in the preferences; otherwise choose the clearest step-by-step demo that covers voltage, current, resistance, continuity, and range/probe selection.
- To copy title reliably, you can right-click the video title and select "Copy" or read the tab label after the page loads.
