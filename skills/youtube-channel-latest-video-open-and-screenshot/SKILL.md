## Steps

1. Open Chrome (use Spotlight: Cmd+Space → type Chrome → Enter) or use the open-app skill if available.
2. Go to youtube.com.
3. In the YouTube search box type the channel name or query (e.g., "Art of Living") and press Enter.
4. Open Filters under the search bar and click the "Channels" filter (TYPE) to limit results to channels. Close the Filters pane if it stays open.
5. From the results, find and click the official/verified channel (look for the channel name and verification badge). If multiple matches, prefer the channel with the verification check or the canonical channel name.
6. On the channel page click the "VIDEOS" tab.
7. If the channel page has a small channel-search box, clear it (click the X) so all videos are visible.
8. Click the "Sort by" control at the top-right of the videos grid and choose "Date added (newest)" (or "Newest").
9. Scan the top of the sorted list for a video matching the requested keywords, presenter name, or showing a LIVE tag. If the task specifies a presenter (e.g., "Mayur Karthik"), look for that name in the title/thumbnail or use the channel-search box to filter to that presenter before sorting, then clear it afterwards if needed.
10. Click the topmost matching thumbnail to open the video page.
11. Start playback and ensure audio is unmuted:
    - Click the play button if needed (or press Space), then click the speaker icon in the player to unmute; or press "m" to toggle mute/unmute.
12. Copy the video's title and URL:
    - Copy URL: focus the address bar (Cmd+L) → Cmd+C.
    - Copy title: select the title text under the player (click to select or triple-click) → Cmd+C (or right-click the title and choose Copy).
13. Capture a screenshot of the video page (player visible and unmuted):
    - To capture the Chrome window: Cmd+Shift+4 → Space → click the Chrome window (saves to Desktop).
    - Or to capture a selected area (player region): Cmd+Shift+4 → drag to select → release (saves to Desktop).
14. Report the copied title and URL and attach the saved screenshot.

## Tips

- Verify the channel by looking for the verification badge (checkmark) and the correct channel name.
- If you need a live session specifically, prefer thumbnails/titles with a LIVE tag and check the stream date/time; live streams often show a red LIVE indicator.
- If the "Sort by" control isn't visible, ensure you are on the Videos tab and that the page is fully loaded; sometimes the control is at the top-right of the grid.
- If the video autoplays muted, press "m" or click the speaker icon to unmute; ensure system volume is up (use set-system-and-youtube-volume skill when consistent volume is required).
- Use Cmd+L then Cmd+C to reliably copy the canonical video URL. If you need the video share URL at a specific timestamp, use the share menu on the player and copy the timestamped link.
- If channel-search narrowing was used, remember to clear it before applying the final "Sort by" so you see all newest uploads.
- When in doubt about whether the channel is official, cross-check the channel URL or search the channel name in web search to confirm.
