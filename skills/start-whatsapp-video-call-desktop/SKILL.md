description: >-
  Start a video call with a contact using WhatsApp Desktop.
---

## Steps
1. Open WhatsApp Desktop (click the Dock icon or launch from Applications / Spotlight).
2. In the left sidebar, find and click the contact (or use the search box at the top of the sidebar and type the contact name).
3. With the chat open, click the video camera icon (video call) in the chat/header area to initiate a video call.
4. If macOS prompts for camera or microphone permissions, click "Allow" to enable audio/video.
5. During the call, use the in-call controls to mute/unmute microphone, disable/enable camera, or end the call (red phone/hang-up button).
6. To leave the call, click the red hang-up button in the call window.
