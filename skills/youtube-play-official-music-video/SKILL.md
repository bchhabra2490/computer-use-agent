  Opens Chrome, searches YouTube for a specified song/artist, selects the official or highest-quality upload (official artist channel / Vevo), starts playback unmuted, and reports the video title and URL. Use when asked to play an official music video or ensure the canonical upload is playing.
---

## Steps
1. Open Google Chrome: press Cmd+Space, type "Google Chrome", press Enter.
2. Focus the address bar: press Cmd+L.
3. Search YouTube for the song: type a query like "<song name> <artist> official" (e.g., "Highway to Hell AC/DC official") and press Enter, or go directly to a YouTube search URL.
4. On the results page, locate and click the top result that appears to be the official upload — prefer the artist's official channel or a Vevo/official channel (channel name will contain the artist or "VEVO"). If multiple official uploads exist, choose the one with the highest resolution/quality and the official channel badge.
5. Mostly, video starts automatically. But confirm by taking screenshot twice and checking the playback time.
6. Ensure the player is unmuted:
   - Click the speaker icon at the bottom-left of the player so the slider is visible and not muted.
   - If the Chrome tab shows a muted icon, right-click the tab and choose "Unmute site."
   - Optionally ensure system volume is audible (use keyboard volume keys or system volume menu).
7. No need to report anything back. Leave the video playing.

## Tips
- If multiple results look official, prefer the one uploaded by the verified/official artist channel or Vevo. Look for the channel name and upload date/quality.
- If the site or tab is autoplay-blocked, clicking play inside the player usually resolves it. If audio remains muted, check right-click on the tab → "Unmute site."
- For consistent loudness across devices, consider using the existing set-system-and-youtube-volume skill before or after starting playback.
- If the requested upload is geo-blocked, pick the highest-quality official mirror or report the block to the user.
